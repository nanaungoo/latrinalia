# User Interview — Latrinalia

## မိတ်ဆွေသုံးသပ်ချက်

---

## Interview Info

- **Who:** Soe Thu Aung — Myanmar guy (Nhar Buu :D ), works in same factory
- **When:** July 2025
- **How:** In person at lunch break — ~15 min

---

## What they do today

After work he go to his room and scroll Facebook and TikTok. Sometimes he watch Myanmar comedy videos and share in Messenger group with friends back home. He don't really post anything, just watch and laugh.

---

## What they liked

- He say "hahaha this is funny bro, like writing on toilet wall"
- He like that nobody know who write it, very free feeling
- He say the dark theme look cool, easy on eyes at night

---

## What confused them

- He try to drag the sticker but cannot move it, say "why not working bro"
- He ask why no emoji, "everyone use emoji on Facebook"
- He ask can he write in Myanmar language, "I want to write to my friends in Myanmar font"

---

## What would make them use it

- If I show him how to use it properly he say he will try more
- If there is emoji and Myanmar font he will definitely use it
- If his friends also use it he will check every day

---

## What I'll change

- [ ] Add a simple instruction pop-up when user first opens the app
- [ ] Add emoji picker so people can use emoji
- [ ] Support Myanmar Unicode font in sticker text
